Reston Hiring Map

Ninety-plus researched employers from Warrenton to Capitol Hill, ranked for a political science grad who wants work without a security clearance. Tune the ranking, open a careers page, or explore the biggest office buildings in 3D.

Getting around

Mouse
Drag to slide the map. Right-drag or Shift-drag to rotate and tilt. Scroll to zoom at the cursor. Double-click to zoom in, Shift-double-click to zoom out.
Touch
One finger slides, two fingers pinch to zoom. Switch the Move button to Turn to rotate and tilt.
Keys
↑↓←→ or WASD move, QE rotate, RF tilt, +− zoom, N north up, T 3D or top-down, H home.
Browse
[ ] or the Prev and Next buttons step through the list. / searches, and Enter flies to the top result.

How matches are ranked

How the match score works: researchers rated each employer 1 to 5 on role fit, no-clearance access and entry-level access, citing the pages in each card. Commute is scored from straight-line distance to your home (Reston until you set it), with a bonus for offices near a Metro station. The score is the weighted average on a 0 to 100 scale. Ratings are judgments from public postings and pages as of November 2026, so check each listing before you apply.

Office sizes are estimates: footprint from OpenStreetMap times tagged floors, in gross square feet. Names in map data can be out of date. Pins are placed from address lookups; a few are placed at street level. Your home point and ranking weights are stored only in this browser.
